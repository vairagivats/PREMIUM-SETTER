FAQ

Straight answers.

Why is the demo so affordable?

It's a launch offer so you can judge our work before spending real money, and so we can earn case studies. Ongoing work is priced higher, and we'll show you that pricing before you decide.

What happens after I pay?

You receive a short intake form about your offer and ideal customer. We confirm the qualification criteria with you, then start outreach within 2 business days.

How fast will I get appointments?

We aim to deliver within 21 days of receiving your completed intake form. Timing can vary with your market and audience, and we'll tell you early if we expect a delay.

What if you can't deliver?

Full refund if we don't deliver your appointment. Email us and we'll process it.

What time zones do you work in?

We're a remote team and schedule calls to fit your hours. Tell us your time zone (ET, CT, MT, PT) on the intake form and we'll book around it.

Is your outreach compliant?

Yes. We follow CAN-SPAM for email, include opt-out options, and honor do-not-contact requests. We don't buy or send to unverified personal lists.

Can this connect to my CRM or calendar?

Meetings are booked directly on your calendar (Google, Outlook, Calendly). CRM handoffs can be set up after the demo.
